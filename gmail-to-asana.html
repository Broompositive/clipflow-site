Save Gmail Emails and Highlighted Text to Asana Tasks in One Click

Highlight text on any page and press Alt+Shift+C: the text becomes the task title. Open an email in Gmail and the subject, sender, link and body become an Asana task. No Google sign-in is involved.

What you need

  • An Asana account and a Personal Access Token (free, takes about 20 seconds).
  • The ClipFlow extension (free during beta).

Step by step

  1. Get your Asana token. Go to app.asana.com/0/my-apps, choose Create new token and copy it.
  2. Connect ClipFlow. Click the ClipFlow icon, open Settings, paste the token under Asana and press Test Connection. Chrome will ask you to allow access to app.asana.com. Click Allow. Nothing else on your browser gets that access.
  3. Add a preset. Open Forms and choose Gmail → Asana Task or Webpage → Asana Task. Pick the Workspace, Project and (optionally) Section where tasks should land. You can also tick Assign to me, set a due date such as +2d, and fill custom fields.
  4. Clip. On a web page, highlight some text and press Alt+Shift+C (or click the toolbar icon). In Gmail, open an email and click Save to ClipFlow.
  5. Tweak and save. Edit the title, notes or custom fields if you like, then press Save Clip (or Ctrl+Enter).

Custom fields work too

If your Asana project has custom fields, ClipFlow shows them right in the popup, with the right control for each type:

Asana field typeWhat you get
DropdownA list of the field's options
Multi-selectTick the options you want
DateA date picker
NumberA number box
TextA text box

Fields you leave blank are simply not sent. Custom fields need your Asana plan to support them on that project.

The variables

VariableWhat it fills in
{{selection}}Text you highlighted (task title falls back to the page title when nothing is highlighted)
{{email.subject}}The email subject
{{email.from}}Sender name and address
{{email.url}}Link back to the thread in Gmail
{{email.body}}Cleaned email text

Is it private?

Yes. ClipFlow talks straight from your browser to Asana using your own token. There is no ClipFlow server, no tracking, and the Gmail button only reads the email you have open, when you click. See the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn a Gmail email into an Asana task?

Install ClipFlow, paste an Asana Personal Access Token in Settings, add the Gmail to Asana Task preset and pick a project. Then open an email in Gmail and click Save to ClipFlow.

Can I create an Asana task from highlighted text?

Yes. Highlight text on any web page and press Alt+Shift+C. The highlighted text becomes the task title; with nothing highlighted, the page title is used.

Does ClipFlow support Asana custom fields?

Yes. Dropdown, multi-select, date, number and text custom fields appear in the popup. Your Asana plan must support custom fields on that project.

Is ClipFlow's Asana integration free?

Yes. ClipFlow is free during the beta with every feature unlocked, and it talks straight from your browser to Asana with your own token.
