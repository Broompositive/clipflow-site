How to Save Gmail Emails to ClickUp Tasks Without Google OAuth or Logout Loops

ClipFlow reads the email you already have open and creates a ClickUp task from it. It never asks for access to your Google account, so there is no Google sign-in to loop or expire.

What you need

  • A ClickUp account and a Personal API Token.
  • The ClipFlow extension (free during beta).

Step by step

  1. Get your ClickUp token. In ClickUp open your avatar menu, then Settings, Apps, API Token, Generate. Copy the pk_... token.
  2. Connect ClipFlow. Click the ClipFlow icon, open Settings, paste the token and press Test Connection.
  3. Add the Gmail preset. Open Forms and choose Gmail → ClickUp Task. Pick the Workspace, Space and List where these tasks should land. Optionally set status, priority, tags and a due date such as {{date+2d}}.
  4. Open an email in Gmail. Click Save to ClipFlow next to the email, or click the toolbar icon. The popup opens already filled in.
  5. Tweak and save. Edit the title or description if you like, then press Save Clip (or Ctrl+Enter).

The email variables

The preset uses these. You can change or reuse them in any form:

VariableWhat it fills in
{{email.subject}}The email subject (default task title)
{{email.from}}Sender name and address
{{email.url}}Link back to the thread in Gmail
{{email.body}}Cleaned email text

The default description is From: {{email.from}}, then the link, then the body. Make your own form to change that.

Why no Google OAuth?

ClipFlow only reads the email that is open on your screen, at the moment you click. It does not request access to your mailbox, so nothing can expire, and nothing is sent to a ClipFlow server. If the Gmail button does not appear, see Support.
