Looking for a faster ClickUp Chrome extension?

If the official ClickUp extension slows your browser or logs you out, ClipFlow is built the other way round: no polling, no always-on scripts on other sites, and your token stays put until you remove it. It only runs when you press Save.

Frequently asked questions

Is ClipFlow free?

Yes. ClipFlow is free during the beta, with every feature unlocked. Install it from the Chrome Web Store.

Which apps can ClipFlow save to?

ClickUp tasks, Asana tasks and Airtable records. It can clip any web page, text you highlight, or the Gmail email you have open.

Does ClipFlow slow down Chrome?

It is built not to. ClipFlow has no background timers or polling, and injects nothing on other sites except a small button on Gmail. It reads a page only when you open the popup and press Save.

Does ClipFlow need access to my Google account?

No. ClipFlow reads the open email from the Gmail page when you click. It does not use the Gmail API or Google OAuth, and there is no ClipFlow server. Your tokens stay in your browser.
