ClipFlow Support

Quick setup help, troubleshooting, and direct contact.

Contact us

Email clipflow.support@gmail.com with your Chrome/Edge version, target app (ClickUp, Asana or Airtable), and a short description of what happened. We reply as quickly as possible.

Common questions

  • Where do I get a ClickUp API token? In ClickUp, open your avatar menu → Settings → Apps → API Token → Generate, then paste the pk_... token into ClipFlow's Settings tab and click Test Connection.
  • Where do I get an Asana token? Go to app.asana.com/0/my-apps, choose Create new token, paste it into Settings under Asana and click Test Connection. Chrome will ask you to allow access to app.asana.com. Click Allow.
  • Where do I get an Airtable Personal Access Token? Open airtable.com/create/tokens, create a token with data.records:write and schema.bases:read scopes, add the bases you want, and paste it into Settings. Works on the Airtable Free plan.
  • I got a 401 error when saving. Open Settings, paste a fresh token, and click Test Connection. If you added new ClickUp lists or Airtable tables recently, click Refresh Workspace Cache.
  • Where is my data stored? Everything stays locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). See our Privacy Policy.
